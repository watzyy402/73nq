Methodology

STDV & OTE.

OTE
Optimal Trade Entry
0.62 — 0.79 Fibonacci

The OTE is the institutional entry window — where algorithms re-engage after sweeping liquidity. When price delivers into 0.62–0.79 of a prior swing after displacement, smart money builds. Wait for the algorithm. Don't chase it.

STDV
Standard Deviation
Algorithmic Delivery Levels

STDV models how far price is programmed to travel from an anchor. ±1, ±2, ±2.5 extensions project exact targets before price arrives. Not probability — the algorithm's math made visible.

Confluence — When they meet, you act

The edge is the intersection. When price retraces into the OTE zone (0.62–0.79) and simultaneously arrives at a ±1 or ±2 STDV extension, the algorithm is printing its footprint. Both must align. If they don't — there is no trade.
