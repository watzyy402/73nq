Methodology

STDV & OTE.

OTE
Optimal Trade Entry
0.62 — 0.79 Fibonacci Retracement

The OTE is the institutional entry window — the zone where algorithms re-engage after sweeping liquidity. When price delivers into 0.62–0.79 of a prior swing after a displacement, that's where smart money builds. Wait for the algorithm to come to you.

STDV
Standard Deviation
Algorithmic Price Delivery Levels

STDV models how far price is programmed to travel from an anchor. Using ±1, ±2, ±2.5 standard deviation extensions, we project exact delivery targets. This is not probability — it's the algorithm's math made visible.

Confluence — When they meet, you act

The real edge is the intersection. When price retraces into the OTE zone (0.62–0.79) and simultaneously arrives at a ±1 or ±2 STDV extension, the algorithm is printing its footprint. Both must align. If they don't — there is no trade.
